Scientific volumes and volume rendering

  • A scientific volume stores one scalar per voxel, for example density, temperature or vorticity, on a regular 3D grid
  • Trilinear interpolation turns the voxel grid into a continuous field v(x) that we can query at any point
  • A transfer function maps each scalar to a colour and an opacity, which decides what the viewer actually sees
  • Direct volume rendering marches rays through the field and composites samples front to back
FRONT-TO-BACK COMPOSITING
C = Σi Ti αi ci
Ti = Πj<i (1 − αj)
ci and αi come from the transfer function at sample i. Ti is the light that survives up to that sample.
WHY IT MATTERS HERE
Every pixel integrates hundreds of samples along a ray. A small jump in the field becomes a visible line in the image, because the transfer function amplifies sharp value changes.
