Preliminaries  07

Quality metrics used in this work

PSNR
PSNR = 20 log10(range) − 10 log10(MSE)
Global over all voxels. Boundary over a 2-cell band around every split. Interior over the rest.
SSIM
Structural similarity of rendered images. It compares local mean, contrast and structure, so it tracks perceived quality better than pixel error.
VALUE JUMP
Jv = mean | fA(x−) − fB(x+) |
Measured on both sides of each interface, normalised by data range.
GRADIENT JUMP
Jg = mean | ∂nf− − ∂nf+ |
Normal-derivative mismatch. Ground truth has a floor of 1.75e-3, since real data also varies there.
