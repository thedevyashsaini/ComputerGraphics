Problem  08

Seams at brick boundaries

From Wurster et al. 2024: 1 ghost cell at 41.59 dB shows a seam, 16 ghost cells at 40.56 dB hide it
  • Two independently trained bricks disagree slightly at the split, so the field jumps
  • Isosurfaces and shading show the jump as a crack or a bright line
  • The paper widens the overlap to 16 ghost cells and accepts about 1 dB less PSNR
  • Their future work lists a communication scheme between bricks
